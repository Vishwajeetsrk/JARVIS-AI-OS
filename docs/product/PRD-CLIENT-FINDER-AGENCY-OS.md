## 3. Core Functional Modules

### 1. Live Client Discovery Feed
- Real-time client opportunities scraped and vetted from Upwork, LinkedIn Jobs, Wellfound, Twitter/X, and direct B2B leads.
- Filterable by skill, budget ($/hr or fixed), client payment verification status, and project duration.

### 2. Proposal & Pitch Studio
- 1-click generation of high-conversion proposals tailored to the client's exact problem statement.
- Automatically inserts relevant project links (`learnifyai.in`, `github.com/Vishwajeetsrk/JARVIS-AI-OS`), delivery timeline, and milestone breakdown.

### 3. Client CRM & Deal Pipeline (Kanban)
- Visual tracking stages: `Prospects` → `Pitch Sent` → `Negotiation` → `Active Project` → `Delivered` → `Paid`.

### 4. Rate Calculator & Service Catalog
- Pre-configured package tiers (Starter, Pro, Enterprise) for instant quoting.

### 5. Invoicing & Revenue Analytics
- Milestone billing, payment status tracking (Cashfree, Razorpay, Stripe, Bank Transfer), and total earned revenue metrics.
