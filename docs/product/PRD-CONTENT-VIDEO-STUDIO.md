# PRD — JARVIS Autonomous Content, Video & Social Studio

> **Status**: APPROVED  
> **Target Release**: JARVIS AI OS v4.0.0  
> **Author**: Vishwajeet Srk  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The JARVIS Content & Video Studio is an autonomous multi-media production suite that takes raw ideas or topics and transforms them into complete video scripts, scene-by-scene storyboards, AI image/thumbnail concepts, SEO-optimized metadata (titles, viral hashtags, structured descriptions), best upload time predictions, and multi-platform distribution across YouTube, Instagram Reels, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X/Twitter.

---

## 2. Core Functional Modules

### 1. Scriptwriting & Storyboard Engine
- **Hook Optimization**: First 3-second hook to maximize viewer retention (>70% retention target).
- **Core Narrative**: High-value educational or entertainment content with technical accuracy.
- **Scene-by-Scene Breakdown**: Scene visuals, on-screen text, B-roll suggestions, and audio voiceover script.
- **Audio Voiceover Preview**: Speech synthesis with pacing and cadence controls.

### 2. Visual & Thumbnail Concept Studio
- **Thumbnail Prompt Generation**: Hyper-specific prompts engineered for Midjourney, DALL-E 3, and Imagen 3.
- **Visual Composition**: High-contrast text overlays, facial expression cues, and color palette tokens.

### 3. Viral SEO & Metadata Optimizer
- **Multi-Platform Title Generator**: High-CTR title variants ranked by search volume.
- **Hashtag Matrix**: Tiered hashtag strategy (Broad #AI, Niche #AgenticWorkflows, Community #BuildInPublic).
- **Structured Description**: Timestamps, resource links, GitHub repos, and call-to-action (CTA).
- **Best Upload Timing Predictor**: Analyzes target audience timezones (e.g. 6:30 PM IST / 9:00 AM EST) for peak organic reach.

### 4. Multi-Platform Scheduler & Publisher
- Unified publishing queue for YouTube Shorts/Long-form, Instagram Reels, X/Twitter Threads, LinkedIn Posts, and TikTok.

### 5. Social Analytics & Revenue Dashboard
- Live tracking of Views, Likes, Comments, Shares, and Engagement Rate.
- Revenue estimator for AdSense, affiliate conversions, and brand sponsorships.
